# Tall-order
Tall Order is a simple, satisfying stacking game that you control with one tap.

A block slides across the screen. Tap to drop it onto the tower below. Anything that hangs over the edge is sliced off, so every miss makes your next block narrower. Line it up perfectly and you keep your full width. Miss completely and the tower topples.

How far can you go?

Features

One-tap controls, easy to learn and hard to put down
Three difficulty levels: Easy, Normal and Hard
Perfect drops start a combo, and three in a row grow your block back
Gentle sound effects that rise in pitch as your combo grows, with an on/off switch
A separate best score for each difficulty
Clean, bold design with colours that shift as your tower climbs
Short rounds, so you can play for a minute or an hour

Choose your challenge

Easy: a wide first block and a slower slide, good for warming up
Normal: the classic balance of speed and skill
Hard: a narrow start, a fast slide and a tight margin for perfect drops

Tall Order is made for quick breaks, commutes and waiting rooms. Beat your best, then try the next difficulty.

Build it tall. Build it perfect.
